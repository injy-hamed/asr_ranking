ASR Transcript Ranking Task

Welcome to this task. You will be shown five sets of sentences, each set containing six outputs from a speech-to-text (ASR) system. Your job is to rank the sentences within each set by how accurately they match what was actually said — from most accurate (Rank 1) to least accurate. You can give the same rank to more than one sentence if they are equally accurate. Once you finish all five sets, your results will be saved automatically.
