ASR Transcript Ranking Task

Welcome to this task. You will be shown around 500 sets of sentences, each set containing six outputs from a speech-to-text (ASR) system. Your job is to rank the sentences within each set by how accurately they match what was actually said — from most accurate (Rank 1) to least accurate. You can give the same rank to more than one sentence if they are equally accurate.
