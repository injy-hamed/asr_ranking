ASR Transcript Ranking Task

This task evaluates the human acceptability of ASR transcripts for utterances containing both Arabic and English words. During the task, you will be presented with 50 audio recordings, one at a time. For each audio recording, you will be shown six transcripts generated by different automatic speech recognition systems. Your task is to rank the transcripts from most acceptable (Rank 1) to least acceptable (Rank 6). Your judgment should be based on:

  • Transcription accuracy: How accurately the transcript reflects the spoken utterance.
  • Script and spelling acceptability: Whether Arabic and English words are written using an acceptable script (Arabic or Latin) and with acceptable spelling.
You may assign the same rank to two or more transcripts if you judge them to be equally acceptable. All transcripts must be assigned a rank, and the assigned ranks must be consecutive, starting from Rank 1.

Your progress is saved automatically after every sample, so you may close the page at any time and continue later, even from a different device. To resume the task, enter the same name exactly as you used previously.
